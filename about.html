About Me

I'm a fullstack developer fueled by curiosity and coffee ☕. Building things from scratch is my happy place 💡 whether it's a slick React/TypeScript interface or a solid Node.js/Express backend. My instructor days taught me that the best code comes from great conversations, so I thrive in collaborative, agile teams where ideas bounce around freely 🤝. Always tinkering with new tech 🚀, always advocating for accessibility and performance, always ready to pair program.

I came to software through physics, then several years of building websites and teaching Java. That path is why I care about both the product and the people building it: clear interfaces, reliable backends, and teams where technical decisions are easy to explain. I am currently deepening that work in a fullstack JavaScript program, with a focus on React, Node.js, and code that stays accessible and maintainable.
